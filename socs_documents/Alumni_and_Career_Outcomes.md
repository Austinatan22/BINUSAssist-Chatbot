# SOCS Alumni & Career Outcomes

> Sources: https://socs.binus.ac.id/computer-science/ (official) and third-party
> aggregation via web search. Retrieved 2026-07-07.
> CAUTION: items marked (unverified) come from third-party/aggregated sources, not an
> official BINUS page — verify with the School before presenting these to prospective
> students. The per-program curriculum catalogs also each contain an official
> "career prospects" section, which is the authoritative source for job roles.

## Career paths (official, from program pages & catalogs)

- Computer Science highlights roles such as **Data Scientist / Data Analyst** and
  **Web Engineer / Developer**, among the role lists in each program catalog.
- Program-specific roles are detailed in each catalog (e.g., Cyber Security → penetration
  tester / security analyst; Game App & Tech → game programmer / designer / artist).

## Testimonial (official, SOCS site)

- *Marie Gray* — gained a deep understanding of cybersecurity and data protection through
  the program; now works as a **Cybersecurity Analyst at a financial company**.

## Employability signals (unverified — third-party aggregation)

- CS-field graduates reported among the higher employability rates, with graduates at
  leading tech companies or founding startups.
- Named examples circulating online include alumni at IBM Indonesia, an iOS Developer at
  CIMB Niaga, and founders/figures linked to East Ventures, Traveloka, Sociolla, IDN Media.
  Treat these as indicative only until confirmed by the School's alumni office.

## Recommended: request from the head of program

Official, current alumni-outcome data (employment rate, median starting salary, top hiring
companies, notable alumni with consent to be named) is the single most persuasive content
for prospective students and is NOT published as a downloadable document — ask the head of
program for it directly.
