# Why SOCS — Facilities & Student Life

> Sources: https://socs.binus.ac.id/ , https://socs.binus.ac.id/computer-science/
> Retrieved 2026-07-07. Some facility/club details below are summarized from the SOCS site
> and general BINUS pages; confirm specifics with the School before publishing to applicants.

## Why choose SOCS

- Curriculum benchmarked to the **ACM Computer Science** standard plus local/foreign
  universities and market trends, so graduates compete nationally and internationally.
- Computer Science offers five specialization streams: **Intelligent Systems, Software
  Engineering, Multimedia Technology, Database Systems, Network Technology**.
- Program mission: students apply their learning to build creative solutions to real
  problems, graduating with both technical and soft skills.
- Curriculum co-developed with renowned industry partners to match workplace expectations.

## Facilities

- Modern computing labs, maker spaces, cloud credits, and standard developer tooling.
  (The SOCS site lists a "Facilities" section; exact lab inventory should be confirmed
  with the School.)

## Student life & communities

- **HIMTI** — School of Computer Science Student Association (Himpunan Mahasiswa Teknik
  Informatika).
- **Student Clubs** — help members develop and refine innovative technology ideas.
- **S Class Program** — a platform to develop new concepts in future technology.
- **Student Products** — hands-on building of creative tech solutions.
- University-wide: BINUS English Club (BNEC), hackathons, startup incubators,
  entrepreneurship support, career fairs, and on-campus recruitment drives.
