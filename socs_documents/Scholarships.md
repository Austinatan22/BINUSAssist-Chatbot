# BINUS Undergraduate Scholarships (relevant to SOCS applicants)

> Sources: https://binus.ac.id/scholarship/list/ , https://binus.ac.id/widia-scholarship/
> Retrieved 2026-07-07. Coverage amounts/percentages change per intake — verify from source
> before quoting to applicants. "Not specified" = amount not published on the list page;
> the detail page or admission office has the current figure.

## SOCS-specific

- **StarTech Scholarship** — the School of Computer Science's flagship scholarship.
  Eligibility: outstanding students, national/international competition participants,
  strong academic/professional potential. Coverage: per current StarTech terms (verify).

## University-wide (open to SOCS applicants)

- **Widia Scholarship** — top-tier flagship. Full scholarship for up to 4 years. For
  applicants with outstanding academic or non-academic achievement at national/international
  level, or Indonesia representatives.
- **TPKS Scholarship** — awarded based on the Tes Potensi Keberhasilan Studi entrance test,
  for SMA/SMK/equivalent students registering in the scholarship period.
- **BINUS International Scholarship** — up to IDR 127.5 million; dual-degree pathway
  (Indonesian Sarjana + international Bachelor's). For applicants who pass the TPKS test.
- **OSIS Scholarship** — 25% discount on the contribution fee for former active OSIS/MPK
  members.
- **BINUSFLUENCER Scholarship** — full scholarship (up to 4 years) for applicants with
  10,000+ Instagram/YouTube/TikTok followers.
- **BINUSIAN Community Scholarship** — for children/siblings of current or former BINUSIANs.
- **BINUS School Scholarship** — for BINUS School graduates.
- **ASO Japan Grant** — for SMA/SMK students aspiring to become engineers.

Full list and application: https://binus.ac.id/scholarship/list/
