# BINUS Undergraduate (S1) Admission Requirements — AY 2026/2027

> Source: https://gabung.binus.ac.id/admission-requirement/ and
> https://gabung.binus.ac.id/admission-requirement/admission-requirement-academic-year-2026-2027/
> Retrieved 2026-07-07. University-wide (not SOCS-specific); School of Computer Science
> falls under the science-focused track.

## Eligibility

- **Science-focused programs (Computer Science, Engineering, Architecture):**
  SMA (IPA) / SMK / Kurikulum Merdeka.
- All-stream programs (Business, Design, Humanities): SMTA / SMK / Sederajat
  (all majors) / Kurikulum Merdeka.

## Entrance Testing

All S1 applicants sit a standardized assessment battery:
- Study skills evaluation
- Logic and abstraction reasoning
- Non-verbal and verbal comprehension

Additional specialized tests by program:
- Architecture — drawing test
- Design programs — imagination / technical drawing tests
- English programs — English proficiency test
- Teacher Education — interview
- **Global Class programs (incl. Computer Science Global Class) — TOEFL**

## Required Documentation

- Health / medical certificates for specific programs (color-blindness clearance for
  Architecture, Design, Biotechnology; health certificate for Hotel Management).
- Computer Science does not require the color-blindness or health certificates.

## Study Duration

- Standard S1 programs: 4–5 years.
- Double degree programs: 5 years.

## Application Procedure (step by step)

> Source: https://gabung.binus.ac.id/admission-procedure

1. **Form fulfillment** — apply at www.gabung.binus.ac.id (application form, photographs,
   plus any program-specific certificates; Computer Science needs none of the medical ones).
2. **Registration type** — choose Regular track or Scholarship track (Partial / Full).
3. **Entrance test (Tes Potensi Keberhasilan Studi / TPKS)** — attend on the scheduled date,
   arrive 30 min early (>30 min late = disqualified). Covers Logic, Abstraction/Non-Verbal,
   and Verbal reasoning, plus extra components for select programs. Results are final.
4. **Result announcement** — check results via the online portal at www.gabung.binus.ac.id.
5. **Re-registration** — on passing, return the re-registration documents via Admisi Online
   or at the Admission office.
6. **Payment & enrollment** — settle fees and finalize enrollment per the re-registration guide.
