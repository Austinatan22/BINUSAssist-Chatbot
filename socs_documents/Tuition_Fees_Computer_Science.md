## Fee Structure (general)

Financial investment is split into:
1. Institutional Development Fee (DP3) — paid once at enrollment.
2. Tuition Fee per semester — fixed component + variable per-SKS (credit) component.
3. Laboratory / equipment fees.

Installment schemes are available for international undergraduate programs.
The tuition-fee page provides a downloadable PDF with the comprehensive per-SKS schedule.
